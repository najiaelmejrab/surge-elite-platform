League Rules & Information

The Surge Elite U17 Championship League features 20 premier teams competing in a 14-game regular season format. Top 8 teams advance to the post-season championship tournament at the Elite Sports Arena.

🏟️ Primary Venue

Elite Sports Arena — Main Court A & Court B

⏱️ Game Format

Four 10-minute quarters, FIBA regulation clock rules

🏆 Playoff Structure

Single elimination top 8 seed bracket playoff
